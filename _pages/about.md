<h2 id="about" style="color: #008c95;">About Me</h2>

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Biomedical Engineering with a research focus on medical image analysis, artificial intelligence, and advanced MRI methodologies. My doctoral research centers on diffusion MRI, with particular emphasis on developing deep learning approaches for diffusion MRI reconstruction, super-resolution, and brain white matter tractography. Through my research, I aim to develop computational methods that improve the quality, robustness, and efficiency of neuroimaging analysis, while addressing challenges associated with high-dimensional diffusion MRI data and the characterization of complex brain white matter structures.  

My broader interests lie at the intersection of artificial intelligence, medical imaging, and biomedical engineering. I also have experience in experimental biomedical research, including studies of photobiomodulation and electromagnetic-field effects on cancer cells, with an emphasis on cellular morphology and quantitative image analysis. I am particularly interested in interdisciplinary research combining MRI physics, image analysis, artificial intelligence, and quantitative modeling to develop reliable computational tools for biomedical research.
